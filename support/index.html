Frequently asked questions

How do I delete my account?

+
In the app, open Profile and tap Delete account at the bottom. This permanently deletes your account, skin log, photos and coach history. If you can no longer open the app, email data@peautrace.com from the address you signed up with and we will delete it within 30 days.

How do I cancel my subscription?

+
Subscriptions are managed by your store. iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Skincare Daily. Android: Google Play → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

I paid but Pro is not showing

+
Sign in with the same account you used to subscribe, open the upgrade screen and tap Restore purchases. Still stuck? Email us with your store receipt.

How do I move to a new phone?

+
Sign in with the same Google, Apple or email account and your log comes with you. If you use the app as a guest, link an account first in Profile, otherwise your data stays on the old phone.

How do I change or turn off reminders?

+
Open Profile → Reminders. You can change times, set quiet hours or turn reminders off.

What does the AI coach see?

+
Only if you turn it on: your message, your skin profile, the last few weeks of check-ins and the names of products on your shelf. Never your photos, email or payment details. You can stop sharing from the coach menu at any time. See the privacy policy for details.

Can I get a copy of my data?

+
Yes. Use Export data in Profile, or email data@peautrace.com.
