Privacy policy

Your Privacy Matters

We are committed to protecting your personal data and ensuring transparency in how we collect, use, and store your information.

Data We Collect

Profile Information

  • What: Name, email, skin type, concerns, and goals
  • Purpose: Essential for app functionality

Skin Log

  • What: Your daily check-ins (skin score, flare zones, factors such as food, sleep, mood, water and period), routines and routine completions, products on your shelf, experiments and notes
  • Purpose: To show your history and find patterns in your own data. It is private to your account and never sold or shared with advertisers

Progress Photos and Profile Pictures

  • What: Photos you take or upload for your skin journey or profile
  • Purpose: Shown back to you only, for example to compare progress. Stored securely in Firebase Storage and deleted with your account

Purchases

  • What: Your subscription status and store purchase receipts (no card details)
  • Purpose: To unlock Pro features. Payments are handled by Apple or Google

Usage Data

  • What: App usage patterns and preferences
  • Purpose: Helps improve app experience

Device Information

  • What: Device type, operating system, app version
  • Purpose: Technical support and compatibility

AI Skin Coach (optional)

The AI skin coach is off until you agree to it in the app. When you use it:

  • What is sent: your chat message, your skin profile (type, concerns, goals), your daily check-ins from the last 3 weeks (skin score, flare zones, factors, sleep, mood, routine) and the names/categories/start dates of products on your shelf.
  • What is never sent: your photos, email address or payment details.
  • Who processes it: Google's Gemini API (paid tier), acting as our processor, to generate the reply. On the paid tier Google does not use this data to train its models. Requests go through our servers; the app never calls the AI directly.
  • Retention: your chat history is stored in your account so the coach can follow the conversation. You can delete it at any time from the coach menu, and it is deleted with your account.
  • Withdrawal: you can stop sharing your log with the AI at any time ("Stop sharing my log with AI" in the coach menu).
  • Not medical advice: the coach provides general educational information and is not a substitute for a dermatologist or doctor.
  • Advertising: coach data is never used for advertising.

Advertising and Consent

Free users may see ads from Google AdMob. In regions that require it we ask for consent (Google's consent form) before showing personalised ads, and on iOS we ask for permission to track via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change your choice in the app's privacy settings. Your skin log is never shared with advertisers.

Regional Compliance

GDPR Compliance (EU Users)

You are located in a region covered by the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). This means you have enhanced privacy rights and we provide additional data protection measures. You can manage your consent preferences in the app.

CCPA Compliance (California Users)

You are located in California, which is covered by the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). This means you have specific rights regarding your personal information, including the right to know what data we collect and how we use it.

General Privacy Notice

We are committed to protecting your privacy regardless of your location. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.
